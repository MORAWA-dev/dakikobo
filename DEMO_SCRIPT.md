# DakiKobo — 60-second demo script

Public app: https://kimcomehome-dakikobo.hf.space/

Use this sequence for reviewers or a short video.

## 1. Open and frame (0–10s)

- Show the landing strip: Burkina Faso crops, source-backed advice, field confirmation.
- Point to **Sources** for evidence limits and privacy notes.
- Point to **Exemples** as quota-safe demos.

## 2. Text agricultural question (10–20s)

- Tap **Semis du mil** (or ask: « Quand semer le mil ? »).
- Show the **Conseil agricole** case card: short answer, evidence, actions, confirmation.
- Show source cards under the answer.

## 3. Fertilizer tool (20–30s)

- Tap **Fumure sorgho** (or ask: « Dose d'engrais pour le sorgho »).
- Emphasize: doses come from the **deterministic engrais tool**, not the LLM.
- Point to the disclaimer: confirm with a local agent.

## 4. Leaf photo screening (30–45s)

- Use the camera button (or the photo example card).
- Show the structured **Cas de terrain - feuille** with observations, possible causes, actions.
- Stress: « Ceci n'est pas un diagnostic ».

## 5. Honest limits (45–60s)

- Ask something off-topic or underspecified.
- Show either:
  - « Je ne sais pas encore… » (no invented answer), or
  - « Je ne peux pas confirmer… » (partial evidence, needs human check).
- Close on: cautious field triage assistant, not a magic chatbot.
