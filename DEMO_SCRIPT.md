# DakiKobo — 60-second demo script

Public app: https://kimcomehome-dakikobo.hf.space/

Use this sequence for reviewers or a short video.

## 1. Open and frame (0–10s)

- Show the landing strip: Burkina Faso crops, source-backed advice, field confirmation.
- Point to **Sources** for evidence limits and privacy notes.
- Point to **1. Contexte parcelle** (crop / stage / place) and optional **Français simple**.
- Point to **Exemples** as quota-safe demos (no live API spend).

## 2. Text agricultural question (10–20s)

- Tap **Semis du mil** (or ask: « Quand semer le mil ? »).
- Show the **Conseil agricole** case card: short answer, evidence, actions, confirmation.
- Show source cards under the answer.

## 3. Institution / acronym (optional 5s)

- Tap **OAPH Burkina** (or ask: « C'est quoi l'OAPH au Burkina Faso ? »).
- Emphasize the **correct expansion**: Offensive Agropastorale et Halieutique 2023-2025.
- Note: not an invented “Office…” name; field doses still need a local agent.

## 4. Fertilizer tool (20–30s)

- Tap **Fumure sorgho** (or ask: « Dose d'engrais pour le sorgho »).
- Emphasize: doses come from the **deterministic engrais tool**, not the LLM.
- Optional: enable **Français simple** then re-ask to show **Mots simples** footnotes.
- Point to the disclaimer: confirm with a local agent.

## 5. Leaf photo screening (30–45s)

- Use the camera button (or the photo example card).
- Show the structured **Cas de terrain - feuille** with observations, possible causes, actions.
- Stress: « Ceci n'est pas un diagnostic ».

## 6. Honest limits (45–60s)

- Tap **Hors sujet** (or ask: « Comment réparer un moteur de voiture ? »).
- Show « Je ne sais pas encore… » (no invented answer).
- Optional: underspecified field question → « Je ne peux pas confirmer… ».
- Close on: cautious field triage assistant, not a magic chatbot.

## Optional extras (if time)

- **CILSS**: regional role, not field rainfall for one plot.
- **Rotation** example: niébé–céréales and azote (IITA-style message).
- **Humidité du sol** with location context (Kaya) for weather signals.
- **Outils** drawer: météo / sol (SoilGrids may be flaky externally).
